01 / UNDERSTANDING THE GENRE

What is shoegaze?

Shoegaze is a genre of alternative music known for its dreamy atmosphere, layered guitar effects and soft, often distant vocals. It creates a wall of sound that can feel both powerful and peaceful.

The name comes from musicians who often looked down towards their guitar pedals while performing. Instead of focusing on a traditional performance, the music creates an immersive experience through layers of sound.

02 / THE SOUND

Elements of the haze.

01

Distorted guitars

Layers of guitar effects create a dense, textured sound that surrounds the listener.

02

Dreamlike vocals

Vocals often blend into the instrumental layers, creating a distant and atmospheric feeling.

03

Emotion and feeling

Shoegaze can communicate emotion through atmosphere, texture and sound rather than relying only on lyrics.

04

Immersive layers

Multiple sounds work together to create an experience that feels expansive and almost dreamlike.

03 / START LISTENING

Find your first sound.

New to shoegaze? Start by listening to different artists and noticing how each one uses guitar effects, vocals and atmosphere.

There is no single way to experience the genre. Some songs feel loud and overwhelming, while others are soft, gentle and almost weightless. Explore different sounds and find what connects with you.
